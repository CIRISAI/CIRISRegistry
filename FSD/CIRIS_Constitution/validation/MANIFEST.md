# CIRIS Constitution — Validation Manifest

Adversarial per-chapter validation of the CIRIS Constitution (CC) against its original sources, per [`RUBRIC.md`](RUBRIC.md). **30 source chapters** (20 CEG §-files + 10 Accord Books), validated by a 30-agent workflow.

## The headline finding

The draft is a **strong, readable skeleton that over-consolidated**. Where it reorganizes and weaves seams it scores well on readability (avg ~4) and C2-superiority. But it **summarized away load-bearing substance**: CEG chapters fail **C1 byte-exact** because the canonical wire encodings (datetime/hex/H3/NodeCode forms, JCS determinism rules, signing preimages) were *referenced* not *reproduced*; Accord chapters fail **C0 fidelity** because the Books were paraphrased and lost normative content. The recurring 'migrated verbatim in Phase 4' deferral is itself the defect: the validators judge the CC as it stands, and the deferred substance is not yet present. **The fix is to execute that migration — reproduce the exact normative content under the (good) readable structure — not to re-summarize.**
