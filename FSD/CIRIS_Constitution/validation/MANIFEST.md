# CIRIS Constitution — Validation Manifest

Adversarial per-chapter validation of the CIRIS Constitution (CC) against its original sources, per
[`RUBRIC.md`](RUBRIC.md). **30 source chapters** (20 CEG §-files + 10 Accord Books). Each validator
fills one row of [`results.csv`](results.csv) and appends its findings block below.

The two challenges, restated: prove the CC is **(1) byte-exact for CEG purposes** and **(2) superior
to the original**, while staying **100% true to content and intent**. Default posture: skeptical.
